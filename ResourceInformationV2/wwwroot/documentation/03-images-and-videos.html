Images and Videos

Open an item and select Image and Video in the sidebar.

Image

  1. Upload an image with the image control.
  2. Enter Image Alternate Text: a short description for people who cannot see the image. Describe the content and purpose of the image.
  3. Enter Image Source: credit or origin of the image.
  4. Select Save.

Video

Enter the video's web address in Video and select Save.

Tips

  • Always provide alternate text for accessibility.
  • Use images you have permission to use, and cite the source.
  • Save after uploading so the image is attached to the item.
